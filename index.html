01 — ABOUT US

Better understood territories.
Better informed decisions.

TERRAVISION Studies & Consulting is a studies and consulting practice based in N'Djamena, Chad. It uses geospatial tools, analytical methods and digital technologies to support a better understanding of territories.

We support public and private organizations, NGOs, development projects and consulting firms with the collection, processing, analysis and communication of data for their work.

Our aim is to provide rigorous, accessible solutions suited to local realities in Chad and beyond.
